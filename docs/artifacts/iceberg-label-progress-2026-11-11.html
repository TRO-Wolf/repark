Why so close to v2

How the number is computed

  • Each gate carries points by size: 1 for a clerk, docs or ruling step; 2 for a small build unit or an audit; 3 for a design-heavy build unit.
  • Done gates count in full. A gate in flight counts at its share of committed work: signed-zero PR A at 60% (four of five build items committed, review not started), LISTVIEW at 15% (table measured, order drafted).
  • Each label carries a 3-point allowance for one fix round after its audit. Each of the four v2 audits so far (10-28, 10-29, 11-01, 11-05) found at least one blocker. The allowance is an assumption, so the bars shade it apart.
  • v3 inherits every v2 row. The plan runs Phase 5 after Phase 3, and the empty-DML, struct and signed-zero tables all measured the same defects on v3 tables.
  • The nanosecond-column and struct-write labels (both EXPERIMENTAL) are separate labels and are not in these numbers.

Pace and forecast

Dates of record have slipped

The 10-26 plan set v2 for 11-10 and v3 core for 11-17. The 11-05 re-audit (five facade blockers) and the 11-08 master plan added the work in the open rows.

Pace on one lane

Five product units merged in four days: #1277 (11-08), #1283 (11-09), #1290 and #1292 (11-10), #1295 (11-11).

Estimate

The v2 remainder is about 22 points: six build units plus the clerk and audit steps, roughly 10 to 15 lane days. That puts v2 near 11-22 to 11-27 if the re-audit finds no more than the allowance. v3 core would follow about a week later. These dates are estimates, not dates of record.

What could move the number

  • AUD-022 and AUD-023 are the hardest open unit. #1267 was paused at 8e929978 after four verifier FAILs. The struct-pairing half has since shipped on its own as #1292.
  • Signed-zero PR A is at its cost bar. The optimization pass brought the synthetic case from 1.0583x to 1.0093x (commit 22bae1d9). The realistic case drew 1.0715x this round, against 1.0256x before. On the 164 cells whose answers do not change it is 1.0189x. The rest of the cost is in the 43 cells that now read the rows Spark reads. If the bar still reads as missed when the builder hands back, it comes to you.
  • LISTVIEW's root cause is not yet found. If it sits inside DataFusion and needs a fork change, the order halts to you.
  • Release vehicle. v1.5.7 carries signed zero and LISTVIEW. AUD-022, AUD-023 and CTAS go to v1.5.8 unless they are ready earlier (Q5). So the label most likely names v1.5.8, or says it covers an unreleased candidate (R3).
  • New audit findings. Each audit so far has added a blocker. A finding beyond the allowance lowers both percentages.
