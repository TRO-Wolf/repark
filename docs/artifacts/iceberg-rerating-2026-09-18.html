Method and limits

  • Same scripts, same oracle. 39 of the rating's probe scripts ran unchanged, one at a time, in 9 minutes, plus the rating's headline replay and count(*) check. Logs were compared line by line with the 2026-09-16 logs after stripping timestamps and ids.
  • Fourteen inline probes no longer exist. The rating ran them from the shell and only their logs survive. A new RePark-only replay covers their cells for NaN, promoted DML and partition sources, column moves, write options, ORC, file size, multi-argument transforms, timestamp_ns, nested DDL, BY NAME and the procedures. It has no Spark twin; expected values are hand-written.
  • Not re-run: the two PyIceberg read probes, the time-travel failure shapes, the conf-door variants of dynamic overwrite, and the pin batteries (CI runs those on every merge). The filter-consistency probe found 41 checks where the rating had 57, because fewer leftover tables existed; 0 inconsistent in both.
  • Local catalogs only. RePark's memory catalog and Spark's Hadoop catalog on one box. Nothing here touched Glue or S3 Tables.
  • One rater. The grades are the orchestrating session's reading of the measurements. No critic reviewed this page.
