Road to v1.0

The exact sequence from tonight to the v1.0 tag, arranged around one operating model: the fleet preps every unit to PR-ready and parks it; you return on your own schedule, merge the parked PRs in their labeled order, and cut each 0.x tag with the publish-pypi runbook. The release machinery is already wired — PyPI trusted publishing, tag-triggered manylinux wheels (wheels.yml / release.yml), the runbook in .agents/skills/publish-pypi/ — so your walkthrough per release is merges + one tag.

The operating model

  • PR parking. Every unit ends as an open, green, audited PR whose body carries merge order N of M for v0.X. As main moves, the orchestrator continuously rebases parked PRs and re-runs the full parity harness before every push — parked never means stale.
  • Worker tiers. Grok 4.6 is every Actor and Critic through the reset window (maxed out, reset, maxed again — the ruling). GLM 5.3 Flash ($40 z.ai budget ≈ thousands of rounds at $0.002–0.006) takes the mechanical fan-outs where its posture fits — flagged per release below. Fable orchestrates: charters, audits, PR assembly, these boards. No Opus 5.
  • Honesty invariants ride every unit: red-first pins, live-oracle measurement with incidental controls, map lockstep, ledger grammar, the comment ban, one Slack message per unit.
  • Owner touchpoints are only: SQM walks of parked PRs, AWS dispatches (environment approval), the per-release tag, and the handful of gate confirms listed at the bottom.
