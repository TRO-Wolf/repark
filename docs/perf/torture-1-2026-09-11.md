Seconds below are wall time measured by a timing harness that drives the same door
helpers the suite cells use — `session.read.parquet` / `session.read.csv(header,
inferSchema)` → `to_arrow()` for the DataFrame door, `createOrReplaceTempView` +
`spark.sql("SELECT * …")` → `to_arrow()` for the SQL door — on the release build,
one warm run over the generated files. A row's `seconds` is the sum of that door's
Parquet and CSV legs; suite cell durations come from the `--durations=0` run.

## S2-12 — `datasets/` coverage measurement and retirement decision

`grep -rn "repark_datasets"` over `python/`, `scripts/`, `Makefile`.

### S2-12 outcome

No family retires this round. `nested` and `secrets` are shape-covered but retained
for outside consumers (D-7's second test fails: `test_datasets_facade.py`,

that ground alone. The coverage gaps above are the shopping list if a later card
extends the torture generators to finish the retirement.
