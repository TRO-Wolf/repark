**Forms** (each in DOUBLE and FLOAT): negative zero `dlit` (`-0.0D`/`-0.0F`), `neg` (`-CAST(0 AS T)`), `str`
(`CAST('-0.0' AS T)`), `arith` (`CAST(0 AS T) * -1`), `nested` (`CAST(CAST('-0.0' AS U) AS T)`); positive zero

T)`), `nested`. The `select` part pairs every negative form with every positive form in both orders, plus the
same-sign diagonal (142 pairs). `context` puts 12 pairs under 8 operators into `WHERE`, `HAVING`, an inner and a
left join condition, and `WHERE x = 1 OR p`. `mixed` compares each form with a column holding `-0.0`, `0.0`, `1.5`,
NULL. `dml` runs `DELETE` (constant and `id <= 2 AND` forms), `UPDATE` and `MERGE … ON t.id = s.id AND p` on v2
copy-on-write and merge-on-read tables. `nan` pairs `CAST('NaN')`, `sqrt(-1)`, `ln(-1)`, `CAST('NaN') * -1`,
`1` and `Infinity`.

**Doors:** `spark-sql` (`spark.sql`), `dataframe` (typed Column API), `fexpr` (`F.expr`), `filter-str`
(`DataFrame.filter(str)`), `native` (`repark.sql`, no ANSI mode; `D`/`F` suffixes are not expressible there).

**Verdicts:** EQUAL, WRONG (both answered, rows differ), REFUSED (RePark refused, Spark answered),
NOT-EXPRESSIBLE. The native door's REFUSED cells are `IF` and `array(...)` (unresolved routines) and native
`MERGE` (unsupported statement); they are door gaps, not this fault.

**Native DML is confounded and excluded from the counts below:** after every native `DELETE`/`UPDATE` the table
reads empty, including where Spark keeps all three rows. That is not a constant-comparison answer; it was not
diagnosed here (the step 0.3 empty-DML wipe recorded under `task/wo/empty-dml-shared-guard-1/` is the likely
cause).
