  census twins, the H5 list and set-operation positions (100) and repros (55), H1 and P15 (old
  commit 5), and the ANSI-off out-of-range cells (old Q6). Lean: the merge gate is "no statement
  main answers is lost". The residue becomes dated expectations with their own cards. H1 and P15

- **Q4 (Q6's reach).** Does Q6's `TIMESTAMP` as the µs wall also bind the Rust `AnsiDialect` door,
