**The change (four parts).**
1. **Pin binding moves out of the maps.** Each ledger's clauses bind in its sidecar `task/ledgers/<bin>/<unit>-pins.md`, one line per clause: `C-NNN: <path>[::<name>], ...` or `C-NNN: none (records)`. `scripts/check_ledger_grammar.py` rule B reads the sidecars; a map no longer counts; a deleted or renamed test goes red. `scripts/migrate_pins_to_sidecars.py` is the one-time, idempotent mover: it wrote 449 sidecars (205 staging, 152 completed, 92 archive) and cut 2,657 citation lines out of 231 maps. It cut citation text only and wrote no map prose.
2. **The lifecycle carries the sidecar.** `scripts/ledger_lifecycle.py` moves `<unit>-pins.md` with its ledger on `move` and `archive` (no map row is made for a sidecar; `archive` files it under the ledger's own date). Sidecars in `completed/` and the archive are not frozen; a ledger row still is.
3. **Lockstep warns.** `scripts/check_map_md.sh` prints `WARNING:` and exits 0 in both modes. `scripts/sync_map_md.py --check` (link validity, duplicate rows) stays a required gate.
4. **A size ratchet.** `scripts/check_map_size.py` fails a tracked map over 200 lines or 16 KiB unless it is an exact-baseline row in `EXCEPTIONS`; an excepted map must equal its baseline, growth fails and an unlowered shrink fails; `--update-baselines` only lowers or deletes rows and refuses a new violator. The ledger-bin maps and the archive-month maps are exempt lifecycle inventories (`EXEMPT`, `EXEMPT_PATTERNS`). It is in `make ci` and as ci.yml's `map.md size ratchet guard` step on pull requests (`.github/` changed only in that step).

**Unchanged.** The owner-ruling block at `AGENTS.md` lines 14 to 20 and `CLAUDE.md` (see clause C-011). The 129 citations left as inert prose (C-012). The `none (records)` clauses (C-013).
