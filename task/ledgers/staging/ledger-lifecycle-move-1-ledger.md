# Unit ledger — LEDGER-LIFECYCLE-MOVE-1 · a ledger move cuts the source row wherever it sits, and leaves docs/history frozen

**Date:** 2026-11-01 · **Branch:** `fix/ledger-lifecycle-move-1` · **Base:** `aa52bd5f` · **Head:** `3afe916a` · **Model:** builder `muse-spark-1.3-contributor` at effort xhigh (round 1: `88e137c2` and `7f55c14e`; round 2: `08420be3` and `74553ca6`; round 3: `3afe916a`); clerk `claude-haiku-5-5` (the records commit); orchestrator `claude-fable-5-1` · **Policy:** [../../../AGENTS.md](../../../AGENTS.md).
**Path:** STANDARD. **risk_tier: standard.**

**Charter.** Two defects were seen by two clerks on the post-merge rounds of RP-63 and RP-64 (main `c154df8a` and `d0562058`). (1) `python3 scripts/ledger_lifecycle.py move <staging ledger> completed` left the source row in `task/ledgers/staging/map.md`, relinked to `../completed/`, and wrote a synthesised row (the ledger's H1 as its description) into `task/ledgers/completed/map.md`. `cut_row` scanned only the rows under `## Contents`, and the staging map holds about 1,100 rows past `## Pointers`. (2) The same move rewrote `docs/history/facade-tests/tests-map-2026-10-30.md`, a frozen history copy, which the clerks restored by hand each time. Round 1 measured defect 2 instead of guessing, and the round halted on the design question the measurement raised. The owner ruled on 2026-11-01, after the orchestrator's recommendation: `docs/history/` is frozen, a ledger move leaves every file under it byte-identical, stale links there to moved task material are history and not findings, and the three checks (the move plan, `ledger_lifecycle.py check` and `check_docs_links.py`) are taught the exemption in this unit. A gate run by the clerk after round 2 found `make check-ledger-grammar` red on three literal `pins:` citations in the round-2 test fixtures, which rule B reads as citations that name no clause; the orchestrator's scripts-lane gate list had omitted that target. Round 3 (`3afe916a`) spells the label apart in the fixtures.

**Fix.** Round 1 (`7f55c14e`): `cut_row` keeps its Contents scan and falls back to a whole-file exact-path scan with a local blank-seam tidy, so a row past `## Pointers` is cut whole and pasted in order; the synthesised row is made only for a rowless source; the sidecar moves rowless. Round 2 (`74553ca6`): `plan` skips every tracked markdown file under `docs/history/` and names each history file that links to a moved file as left frozen, which `move` and `archive` print; `check` skips `docs/history/` in its dead-link scan; `check_docs_links.py` drops a `does not exist` finding only in a history file whose target is under `task/ledgers/` or `task/roadmap/`. Round 3 (`3afe916a`): the fixtures build their `pins:` lines from a module-level label, so the fixture text reaches the temporary repository unchanged while the source no longer matches the grammar gate's citation pattern.

**Not in this unit.** The stale links inside `docs/history/` are left as written by the ruling, not repaired. The verification of the head (C-014) is owed.
