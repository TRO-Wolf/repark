**Path:** STANDARD. **risk_tier: standard.**

**Charter.** Two defects were seen by two clerks on the post-merge rounds of RP-63 and RP-64 (main `c154df8a` and `d0562058`). (1) `python3 scripts/ledger_lifecycle.py move <staging ledger> completed` left the source row in `task/ledgers/staging/map.md`, relinked to `../completed/`, and wrote a synthesised row (the ledger's H1 as its description) into `task/ledgers/completed/map.md`. `cut_row` scanned only the rows under `## Contents`, and the staging map holds about 1,100 rows past `## Pointers`. (2) The same move rewrote `docs/history/facade-tests/tests-map-2026-10-30.md`, a frozen history copy, which the clerks restored by hand each time. Round 1 measured defect 2 instead of guessing, and the round halted on the design question the measurement raised. The owner ruled on 2026-11-01, after the orchestrator's recommendation: `docs/history/` is frozen, a ledger move leaves every file under it byte-identical, stale links there to moved task material are history and not findings, and the three checks (the move plan, `ledger_lifecycle.py check` and `check_docs_links.py`) are taught the exemption in this unit. A gate run by the clerk after round 2 found `make check-ledger-grammar` red on three literal `pins:` citations in the round-2 test fixtures, which rule B reads as citations that name no clause; the orchestrator's scripts-lane gate list had omitted that target. Round 3 (`3afe916a`) spells the label apart in the fixtures.

**Fix.** Round 1 (`7f55c14e`): `cut_row` keeps its Contents scan and falls back to a whole-file exact-path scan with a local blank-seam tidy, so a row past `## Pointers` is cut whole and pasted in order; the synthesised row is made only for a rowless source; the sidecar moves rowless. Round 2 (`74553ca6`): `plan` skips every tracked markdown file under `docs/history/` and names each history file that links to a moved file as left frozen, which `move` and `archive` print; `check` skips `docs/history/` in its dead-link scan; `check_docs_links.py` drops a `does not exist` finding only in a history file whose target is under `task/ledgers/` or `task/roadmap/`. Round 3 (`3afe916a`): the fixtures build their `pins:` lines from a module-level label, so the fixture text reaches the temporary repository unchanged while the source no longer matches the grammar gate's citation pattern.

**Not in this unit.** The stale links inside `docs/history/` are left as written by the ruling, not repaired. The verification of the head (C-014) is owed.

**Critic round 1 (FAIL, 2026-11-02, on `a0eeac91`).** The Muse MAX critic returned FAIL with four findings, each reproduced by the orchestrator on the same head with the critic's probe harness before the fix round. F1 (S1): a bullet in row form inside an HTML comment (`<!-- ... -->`) above the real tail row was cut by the whole-file fallback and pasted into the destination as the ledger's row, the comment gutted, and the real row was left in staging relinked to `../completed/`. F2 (S1): a prose bullet under a Notes section (`- See [m1-demo-ledger.md](m1-demo-ledger.md) for the background.`) was cut and pasted as the row, and the real row was left behind. F3 (S2): a row-form line inside a fenced block was cut out of the fence. F4 (S2): `move` and `archive` of a source under `docs/history/` raised an unhandled `KeyError` (the history skip in `plan` left `texts[new]` unset), with nothing written. The root cause of F1 to F3 is that the fallback matched any top-level bullet whose first link resolved to the moved path, with no fence or comment masking and no row-form check. The critic's claims 3, 4 and 5 (pins real and red first, gates and hygiene, records true) were PROVEN; claim 2 (history frozen) was PROVEN except for F4.

**Fix round 1 (2026-11-02, on `6f599121`).** Two Muse commits. `f0ad3f2d` adds five pins, red on `a0eeac91` (five failed) and green at head: `test_move_skips_a_commented_bullet_and_cuts_the_real_row`, `test_move_with_only_a_commented_bullet_synthesises`, `test_move_skips_a_prose_bullet_and_cuts_the_real_row`, `test_move_skips_a_fenced_bullet_and_cuts_the_real_row` and `test_move_and_archive_refuse_a_history_source`. `6f599121` is the fix: `_masked_lines` now blanks HTML comments across lines as it blanks fences, so the fallback, the link rewrite and the dangling scan all skip text inside `<!-- -->`; the fallback takes only a bullet in row form (`- [<basename>](` with the old or the new basename as the link text, first on the line) outside fences and comments; `cut_row` takes the old and the new paths; `plan` reads `texts.get(new, "")` for the synthesised row; `run_move` and `run_archive` refuse a source under `docs/history/` with `ERROR: <path> is under docs/history/ — history is frozen`, exit 2, nothing written. The recorded reach beyond the fallback: a link inside an HTML comment is neither rewritten by a move nor counted as dangling by the lifecycle script, while `check_docs_links.py` still checks it. No tracked markdown file holds a ledger or roadmap link inside an HTML comment today (measured by the orchestrator). The orchestrator's gates on `6f599121` exit 0 (the four test files, 78 passed, and the records and lint gates); the critic's F1 and F2 probes now cut the real row and leave the comment and the prose bullet; F4 prints the refusal, exit 2, and leaves the working tree unchanged; the zone-seam-1 dry run is unchanged (moved 2, rewrote 4, wrote 5; history untouched; row cut and pasted whole). CI on the new head and the critic round 2 are owed, so C-014 stays OPEN.
