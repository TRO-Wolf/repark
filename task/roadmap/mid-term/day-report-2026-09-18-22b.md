## 3. The #676 segfault, plainly

After the mechanical rebase, CI's required smoke job (the whole facade suite against the **debug** wheel) crashed 4 xdist workers with `SIGSEGV` in `test_perf_approxpct_1.py`. Those cells are 1,000-branch `UNION ALL` statements. Main was green on the same job.

- **Root cause.** The Opus round measured it with gdb. The unit's column repair cloned the statement before planning. sqlparser's derived `SetExpr::clone` recurses about 17 KB per UNION level in debug. That is about 120 levels on a 2 MiB tokio stack and about 490 on the 8 MiB Python main thread.
- **Fix.** The repair future runs on a stack grown to the statement's measured depth (`stacker`, already in Cargo.lock; the lock gains one edge). The two hand-written recursive walks are now loops.
- **Pins.** Red-first Rust pins on a 2 MiB thread, mutation-proven. A 5,000-branch pin plans; main cannot plan that depth on debug in either case mode.
- **The run-21b "hang at 99 %"** did not reproduce. The cohort ran in 18 s. The likeliest explanation stays the 500-table perf test (21c), which #699 put behind a `perf` marker.
- **Upstream note.** sqlparser's `Spanned::span` walk is unguarded and quadratic inside `set_expr_to_plan`.
