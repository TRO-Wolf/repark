# Card STAMP-2-R5P6-2: the remaining r5p6 construction cost

**Date:** 2026-10-07. **Filed by:** Claude (Opus 5.5), orchestrator. **Source:** the STAMP-2-R5P6-1
attribution (`task/ledgers/staging/stamp-2-r5p6-1-ledger.md` C-005, §6) and the 2026-10-07 gate
record in [perf-attr-stamp-2-card-2026-10-03.md](perf-attr-stamp-2-card-2026-10-03.md).

**Status:** filed, not scheduled. The STAMP-2 gate is **met** (work-equal like median 1.0812,
release builds, quiet box), so this card is not a release gate. It narrows the gap that remains on
one family.

## Why

**The current number.** After #980, family r5p6 still runs at **1.135** against main on the gate
record (12,272 cells, 19.49 s against 17.17 s, an excess of 2.32 s). That is the largest share of
the 2.97 s whole-like excess.

**Where the time goes.** The attribution measured it as **construction cost, not execution**, in
three places. None of them is a twin-search or lineage-trace cost, which the ruled STAMP-2-R5P6-1
target named and measured at under 1 ms.

| Source | Measured cost (STAMP-2-R5P6-1 attribution) |
|---|---|
| The join doors' SQL re-plans through `sql_built` | `j_alias` constructor +1.3 ms, `j_cross` +0.6 ms per constructor; `_join_on_condition_h1` 112 → 466 calls |
| The per-frame attribute-id stamp (`stamp_attribute_ids` → `repark_core::frame_names::stamp`) | about 0.3 s across the like set (7 µs per frame), plus `hashbrown::RawTable::clone` of field metadata maps (+0.20 s self) |
| The binding layer's per-column resolution (`_column_of`, `_bind_resolved_name`, `_rebind_qualified_refs`, the weak-map bookkeeping) | about 1 s spread thin |

## The ask

Apply the rule that #980 proved, **plan natively when the answer is already settled**, to the
join doors. Then measure whether stamping and binding can be made cheaper without changing a single
answer.

- **The join doors.** `crossJoin` and the alias join now route through
  `_join_on_condition_h1` and `sql_built`, where main built them natively. Take the native route
  when the join's column references are attribute-exact, using #980's predicate and its CAST and
  literal discipline. Otherwise keep the SQL route unchanged.
- **The stamp.** Measure whether field-metadata maps can be shared (an `Arc`) instead of cloned
  per schema rebuild, and whether `plan_is_stamped` can short-circuit more frames.
- **Binding.** Profile first. This is the smallest per-call cost and the widest spread, so it moves
  only if a measured hot spot appears.

## Gates

- **Answers:**
  - the replay corpus shows zero deterministic changes;
  - the attr-id, sort and fill suites are identical;
  - the 394-cell sort grid is identical;
  - an Opus verifier differential on every native route against its SQL route, as #980 had.
- **Speed:** a quiet release three-run with the STAMP-2 harness reports the whole like-set ratio and
  the r5p6 family ratio against the same-day main. The whole like set must not regress past the
  1.0812 record.
- The usual Rust and Python gates, plus the comment ban.
